# 纹兽战记 · 该做视频的部分 + 生成提示词

> ⚠️ 版权:提示词用"外形描述"写,括号标注对应宝可梦供**私用**。要公开发布请换原创外形。
> 🎬 工具:Kling / 海螺 / Runway / Veo 均可。**推荐先文生图出关键帧,再图生视频**(角色一致性更好)。
> 统一风格后缀(每条都加):
> `cinematic anime fantasy, painterly, vibrant colors, volumetric lighting, dramatic, monster-taming RPG, highly detailed, 16:9, smooth camera --no text, watermark, logo, subtitles`

优先级:**① 开场 ② 三 Boss 登场 ③ 结局** 最值;④ 三章环境 为锦上添花。

---

## ① 开场 CG(最重要)· 5–8s
用途:标题画面 / 进游戏前。情绪:孤独、宿命、再次出发。
```
A small fox-like creature with brown fur and a large fluffy cream-colored neck ruff (Eevee) slowly opens its glowing eyes in a dim ancient ruin; faint glowing tattoo-marks pulse across its body and float in the air like embers; it stands and looks toward a distant abyss of swirling psychic light; lonely, fated, hopeful; slow push-in camera
```

## ② Boss 登场 CG(三条,各 4–6s)

**暴鲤龙(章1·暴怒)**
```
A massive serpentine blue sea-dragon (Gyarados) erupts from a calm misty bay, water exploding around it, fanged maw roaring, scales flushing red with rage, lightning in storm clouds; low-angle hero shot, dynamic
```
**快龙(章2·守关古龙)**
```
A large orange dragon with small wings (Dragonite) stands guarding a high windswept mountain ridge at golden hour, arms crossed, calm and immovable, clouds rolling past below; slow orbit camera, majestic
```
**超梦(章3·终焉)**
```
A tall pale psychic feline humanoid with a long purple tail (Mewtwo) floats in a surreal void of fractured mirrors and violet light, opening glowing eyes, weaving a shimmering telekinetic shield of energy rings; ominous, godlike, slow zoom
```

## ③ 结局 CG(胜利)· 5–8s
用途:通关结算。情绪:释放、被记住的不会消失。
```
A dreamlike world shattering like glass as warm golden light pours through the cracks; countless glowing tattoo-marks rise into the sky like fireflies and form silhouettes of many creatures; the small fox creature (Eevee) stands in the light looking up, tearful and free; uplifting, emotional, slow upward camera
```
(可选·失败/循环:`the same small creature dissolves into drifting embers and reawakens at the start, a melancholy time-loop, muted cold light`)

## ④ 三章环境空镜(各 3–5s,做章节过场底图)
```
静水湾: a tranquil misty bay at dawn, calm turquoise water, soft reeds, distant mountains, peaceful, slow drifting camera
龙脊山: jagged dragon-spine mountains piercing through clouds, harsh wind, eagles, epic scale, sweeping aerial camera
心智深渊: a surreal psychic abyss, floating broken islands, violet nebula light, drifting geometric shapes, eerie dreamlike, slow descent camera
```

## 关键帧静图提示词(图生视频前先出图,可选)
把上面任一条去掉运镜词(push-in/orbit/zoom),加 `key visual, concept art, centered composition` 先出静图,满意再图生视频。

## 用法建议
- 时长 4–6s 足够当过场;开场/结局可 8s。
- 比例 16:9(标题/宣传);若要塞进当前网页过场,也可 1:1 或竖屏看你排版。
- 一次多抽几条挑最好的;免费档有水印,介意就用付费单次。
- 拿到 mp4 后,放进游戏的方式我可以再帮你接(过场覆盖层支持播放视频)。
